Validation Summary

AVOutput exposes RDK picture and video-output controls through a Thunder plugin entry point, with TV and STB implementations selected at build time (ARCHITECTURE.md#L5-L7). Of 30 findings, 10 are confirmed, 8 partially valid, 10 false positives, and 2 need more context. The strict false-positive rate is 33.3% (10/30), counting only FALSE POSITIVE results.

The advisor identifies concrete risks in error propagation, unsynchronized TV state, worker/TV teardown ordering, legacy JSON-RPC helpers, and hardcoded paths. It overstates several style preferences and conflicts with repository-specific constructor-time JSON-RPC/IARM setup and destructor cleanup guidance (Plugin.instructions.md#L37-L79, ARCHITECTURE.md#L43-L58). HAL callback lifecycle and build variables require external SDK/build context.
