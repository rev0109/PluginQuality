Validation Summary

HdmiCecSource exposes HDMI-CEC control and status to Thunder clients, bridging JSON-RPC/COM-RPC to CEC, IARM, DeviceSettings, and PowerManager integrations. Validation: 12 confirmed, 10 partially valid, 4 false positives, and 5 needing more context. Definite false-positive rate: 4/31 = 12.9%; partially valid findings are not counted as definite false positives. Source: ARCHITECTURE.md#L5, ARCHITECTURE.md#L57.

The advisor found meaningful lifecycle, error-reporting, observer-concurrency, and OS-synchronization concerns. It overreached on registration semantics, implied raw threads despite Utils::ThreadRAII, and presented preferences or SDK-dependent conventions as established defects. Several lifecycle findings describe one profile-dependent early return, not independent incidents. No build, test, SDK-header inspection, or device reproduction was performed.
